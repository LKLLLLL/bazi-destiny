BaZi Destiny ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website and services.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you use BaZi Destiny, we may collect the following information:

- **Birth Information:** Date of birth, birth time (hour), and birth location that you voluntarily provide for generating your BaZi reading.
- **Name:** Any name you choose to associate with your reading.
- **Usage Data:** Basic analytics about how you interact with our website, such as pages visited and features used.
- **Payment Information:** If you purchase a premium reading, your payment is processed by PayPal. We do not store your credit card or bank details.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To generate and deliver your personalized BaZi reading
- To improve our services and user experience
- To process one-time purchases and deliver paid readings

## 3. Data Storage & Security

Your birth data and reading results remain in your browser unless you choose to share them. For a purchase, we store the PayPal order ID, product tier, amount, currency, and payment status so that we can verify access and prevent an order from being reused. We use industry-standard encryption (TLS/SSL) to protect data in transit. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

## 4. Cookies

We use essential HttpOnly cookies to maintain checkout state and remember access to a purchased reading. These cookies are required for paid features and are not used for advertising or cross-site tracking.

## 5. Third-Party Services

- **PayPal:** Handles all payment processing. Their privacy policy applies to payment data.
- **Google Fonts:** Used for typography. Their privacy policy applies.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data at any time. Contact us at privacy@mybazidestiny.com.
