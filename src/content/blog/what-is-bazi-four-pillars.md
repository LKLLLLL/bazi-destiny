## What Does BaZi Mean?

BaZi (八字), literally "Eight Characters" in Chinese, is an ancient system of Chinese astrology also known as the Four Pillars of Destiny. It uses your birth date and time to create a chart that reveals your personality, strengths, weaknesses, and life potential.

## The Four Pillars Explained

Your BaZi chart consists of four pillars, each containing two characters (one Heavenly Stem and one Earthly Branch):

- **Year Pillar:** Represents your outer world, social identity, and ancestral influence
- **Month Pillar:** Governs your career, ambitions, and relationship with authority
- **Day Pillar:** The most important — represents your core self, spouse, and inner nature
- **Hour Pillar:** Relates to your children, aspirations, and hidden desires

## The Five Elements in BaZi

Every character in your chart is associated with one of the Five Elements (Wu Xing):

- **Wood (木):** Growth, creativity, flexibility
- **Fire (火):** Passion, leadership, charisma
- **Earth (土):** Stability, nurturing, practicality
- **Metal (金):** Precision, discipline, strength
- **Water (水):** Wisdom, adaptability, intuition

The balance of these elements in your chart determines your personality traits and life patterns.

## How is BaZi Different from Western Astrology?

While Western astrology uses the position of planets at your birth, BaZi focuses on the cyclical patterns of time itself. It uses a 60-year calendar cycle combining the 10 Heavenly Stems and 12 Earthly Branches. BaZi is particularly strong at predicting timing — when opportunities and challenges are most likely to occur.

## Common BaZi Terms You Should Know

- **Day Master (日主):** The most important element in your chart — represents YOU
- **Ten Gods (十神):** Ten relationship patterns that describe how elements interact
- **Luck Pillars (大运):** 10-year cycles that show your life phases
- **Useful God (用神):** The element you need most for balance and success
