## Rat + Dragon: A Powerful Match

In Chinese astrology, the Rat and Dragon form one of the most harmonious pairings. They belong to the same compatible group (三合) along with the Monkey, creating a natural understanding between them.

## Love Compatibility: ★★★★★

The Rat and Dragon make an exciting and supportive romantic pair:

- **Mutual admiration:** The Rat is drawn to the Dragon's charisma and ambition, while the Dragon appreciates the Rat's intelligence and resourcefulness
- **Complementary strengths:** Dragon provides vision and courage; Rat provides practical support and attention to detail
- **Emotional harmony:** Both value loyalty and are willing to invest in the relationship
- **Potential friction:** The Dragon's need for freedom may sometimes clash with the Rat's desire for security

## Work Partnership: ★★★★☆

Together they can achieve great things professionally:

- **Dragon leads:** Provides big-picture vision and takes bold risks
- **Rat supports:** Handles details, manages resources, and identifies opportunities
- **Combined result:** Ambitious plans executed with precision

## Friendship: ★★★★★

As friends, Rat and Dragon enjoy a stimulating and loyal bond. They inspire each other to grow and rarely bore each other.

## Tips for Rat-Dragon Relationships

- Rat: Give Dragon space to pursue their big dreams
- Dragon: Appreciate Rat's practical wisdom and don't dismiss their caution
- Both: Communicate openly about expectations and boundaries
