**Against the Current** is the English release title of the 2026 costume drama **兰香如故** (*Lán Xiāng Rú Gù*). Its heroine's names are unusually important to the premise: **沈嘉兰 (Shěn Jiālán)** takes on the identity **许兰香 (Xǔ Lánxiāng)**. The Chinese title echoes *Lanxiang*, making the names more than decorative labels.

The identities and their spellings come from [WeTV's English series description](https://wetv.vip/en/play/94jt6sxiwsjw5n6) and [its Chinese listing](https://wetv.vip/zh-cn/play/94jt6sxiwsjw5n6). The character meanings below are linguistic readings, **not official translations or claims about the writers' private intentions**. This guide discusses the premise but avoids later plot developments.

## What Does 兰香如故 Mean?

The title can be unpacked without forcing it into the English marketing title:

- **兰 (lán):** an orchid or another fragrant *lan* plant in older Chinese usage;
- **香 (xiāng):** fragrance or aroma;
- **如故 (rú gù):** as before, unchanged.

A natural interpretive rendering is **“the orchid fragrance remains as before.”** This is not the official English title: the show is released internationally as *Against the Current*. The [Taiwan Ministry of Education's dictionary](https://dict.revised.moe.edu.tw/dictView.jsp?ID=136353&la=0&powerMode=0) confirms the “as before” sense of 如故.

There is a botanical nuance here. Translating 兰 simply as “orchid” is convenient, but [historical dictionary entries](https://www.zdic.net/hans/%E8%98%AD) also use it for fragrant herbs that are not identical to the modern ornamental orchid. That broader aromatic association matters more than identifying one exact flower species in a fictional name.

## Shen Jialan: 沈嘉兰

**Pinyin:** Shěn Jiālán  
**Surname:** 沈 (*Shěn*)  
**Given name:** 嘉兰 (*Jiālán*)

WeTV identifies **Shen Jialan** as the heroine's original family identity. **沈** is her surname; it should be kept as *Shen*, not translated as a descriptive word. **嘉** commonly suggests something [good, fine, or commendable](https://dict.revised.moe.edu.tw/dictView.jsp?ID=5498&la=0&powerMode=0). **兰** brings fragrant-plant imagery and a classical, cultivated register.

Read as a name, **嘉兰** suggests an admired *lan* flower or herb. It is not a fixed English sentence, and the individual characters do not prove anything about the character's fate or personality. Its value to the story lies partly in belonging to the Shen family: changing the surname changes the identity others see.

## Xu Lanxiang: 许兰香

**Pinyin:** Xǔ Lánxiāng  
**Surname:** 许 (*Xǔ*)  
**Given name:** 兰香 (*Lánxiāng*)

According to WeTV's synopsis, Jialan assumes the identity of **Xu Lanxiang** after her family is destroyed. **许** is the surname of the identity she takes. **兰香** combines the fragrant plant with **香**, “fragrance,” making the floral image explicit. The title **兰香如故** then repeats her assumed given name as its opening two characters.

That overlap allows a reader to hear both a literal fragrance image and a reference to the heroine. It does **not** mean that the production has published one definitive symbolic interpretation. Names in fiction can carry several associations at once.

One detail matters for English readers: **沈嘉兰 and 许兰香 are two different full names**, not alternate romanizations of the same Chinese characters. The shift from *Shen* to *Xu* is a family-name shift; the shared **兰** links the given names visually and aurally.

## Lin Jinqi: 林锦岐

**Pinyin:** Lín Jǐnqí  
**Surname:** 林 (*Lín*)  
**Given name:** 锦岐 (*Jǐnqí*)

WeTV names **Lin Jinqi** as the male lead. **林** is a surname. **锦** can refer to [brocade](https://dict.revised.moe.edu.tw/dictView.jsp?ID=5874&la=1&powerMode=0) and, by extension, something splendid or ornate. **岐** is associated with [branching paths and the place-name Qishan](https://dict.revised.moe.edu.tw/dictView.jsp?ID=6255&la=0&powerMode=0); it should not automatically be read as a prediction that the character faces a “fork in the road.”

**锦岐** has a polished, literary sound suitable for the drama's setting. As with 嘉兰 and 兰香, a character-by-character gloss is useful context, not a certified translation of the complete fictional name.

## Can a C-Drama Name Work in Real Life?

The best lesson from *Against the Current* is how a name can carry **sound, image, family identity, and story role** simultaneously. Borrow that method before borrowing any exact name.

If you like **兰** or **香**, check the complete name aloud with its surname. Consider tone flow, unintended homophones, whether the botanical image feels natural in present-day life, and whether you want a feminine or more neutral impression. A screenwriter can choose a striking name for a plot; a real person needs a name that works every day.
