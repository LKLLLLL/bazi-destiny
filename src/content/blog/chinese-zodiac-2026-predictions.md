## General 2026 Energy

Fire Horse energy is **explosive and liberating**. Obstacles dissolve. Bold moves succeed. The year favors those who act with courage and embrace change. Momentum is everything — those who hesitate will be left behind.

- **Freedom:** Breaking free from constraints — career changes, relocations, and bold life decisions favored

- **Passion:** Intense emotional energy — love and creativity surge, but tempers can flare
