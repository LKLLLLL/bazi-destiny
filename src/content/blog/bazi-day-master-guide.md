## What is the Day Master?

The Day Master (日主 or 日元) is the most important element in your BaZi chart. It represents YOU — your core personality, how you interact with the world, and your fundamental nature. It is determined by the Heavenly Stem of your Day Pillar.

## The 10 Day Masters

There are 10 possible Day Masters, each corresponding to a combination of element and polarity:

### 甲 Jia — Yang Wood 🌲

The Big Tree. Strong, upright, and determined. Natural leaders who stand tall through any storm. Tend to be rigid but incredibly reliable.

### 乙 Yi — Yin Wood 🌿

The Vine. Flexible, graceful, and adaptable. Bends without breaking. Excellent at navigating complex social situations.

### 丙 Bing — Yang Fire ☀️

The Sun. Radiant, generous, and warm. Lights up every room. Natural performers and inspirational leaders.

### 丁 Ding — Yin Fire 🕯️

The Candle. Warm but focused. Illuminates small spaces brilliantly. Detail-oriented and quietly passionate.

### 戊 Wu — Yang Earth ⛰️

The Mountain. Solid, dependable, and strong. The foundation everyone relies on. Patient and trustworthy.

### 己 Ji — Yin Earth 🌾

The Farmland. Nurturing, fertile, and supportive. Grows whatever is planted. Excellent at cultivation and patience.

### 庚 Geng — Yang Metal ⚔️

The Sword. Sharp, decisive, and strong-willed. Cuts through nonsense. Natural protectors and enforcers.

### 辛 Xin — Yin Metal 💎

The Jewel. Refined, precious, and elegant. Values quality over quantity. Beautiful under pressure.

### 壬 Ren — Yang Water 🌊

The Ocean. Vast, deep, and powerful. Contains multitudes. Natural philosophers and strategists.

### 癸 Gui — Yin Water 💧

The Rain. Gentle but persistent. Nurtures everything it touches. Highly intuitive and perceptive.
