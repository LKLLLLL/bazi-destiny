## What Does Wood Element Mean?

In Chinese Five Element theory (Wu Xing), Wood represents growth, expansion, and new beginnings. Like a tree, Wood energy pushes upward and outward, constantly seeking to grow while remaining rooted.

## Wood Element Personality Traits

- **Positive:** Visionary, creative, compassionate, resilient, idealistic
- **Negative:** Stubborn, impatient, overly ambitious, self-critical
- **Decision style:** Long-term thinker, strategic planner
- **Social style:** Warm, encouraging, natural mentor

## Best Careers for Wood Types

Wood types thrive in careers that involve growth, creativity, and helping others expand:

- 🎨 Creative fields: Design, writing, art, architecture
- 🌱 Growth roles: Education, coaching, consulting
- 🌿 Nature-related: Environmental science, agriculture, forestry
- 💼 Leadership: Management, entrepreneurship, strategy

## Wood Element Love Compatibility

- **Best matches:** Fire (Wood feeds Fire) and Water (Water nourishes Wood)
- **Challenging matches:** Metal (Metal cuts Wood) — requires growth through friction
- **Neutral:** Earth (Wood breaks through Earth) — dynamic but manageable

## Health and Wellness for Wood Types

Wood element governs the liver and gallbladder in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Wood types should:

- Manage anger and frustration — these emotions weaken the liver
- Exercise regularly — movement helps Wood energy flow
- Spend time in nature — forests and green spaces replenish Wood energy
- Eat leafy greens and sour foods to support liver health

## Feng Shui for Wood Element

- Place green plants in the East sector of your home
- Use wooden furniture and decor
- Incorporate rectangular shapes
- Use green and blue color palettes
