## Dragon + Rooster: Ambition with Precision

Dragon and Rooster are a traditional **Six Harmony (六合)** pair. The Dragon is associated with vision, confidence, and a willingness to take up space; the Rooster with precision, presentation, and an eye for what can be improved. Their connection can be productive and magnetic, provided admiration does not turn into a contest for control.

## Love Compatibility: ★★★★☆

This pair often recognizes excellence in the other:

- **Mutual admiration:** Dragon brings a sense of possibility, while Rooster notices the details that make a dream credible.
- **Visible commitment:** Both may enjoy a relationship that feels purposeful, well cared for, and proud without needing to be secretive.
- **Useful contrast:** Rooster can refine Dragon's plans; Dragon can keep Rooster from shrinking a bold idea before it begins.
- **Potential friction:** Dragon may hear advice as a challenge, while Rooster may become overly exacting when anxious.

Compliments should be as specific as criticism. The Dragon needs room to lead without being managed, and the Rooster needs evidence that their care is valued rather than merely used. Shared goals help channel their strong standards into cooperation.

## Work Partnership: ★★★★★

This can be a formidable professional pairing:

- Dragon sets direction, attracts attention, and accepts a calculated risk.
- Rooster checks details, improves the presentation, and catches errors before they become public.
- Their combined energy suits launches, events, entrepreneurship, and projects where both vision and polish matter.

Assign decision rights early. If both people believe they are the final editor, small choices can become status battles. A clear review process lets Rooster protect quality and Dragon protect momentum.

## Friendship: ★★★★☆

Dragon and Rooster friends can motivate each other to dress up an idea and bring it into the world. Rooster may offer candid feedback that other friends avoid, while Dragon makes the friendship feel expansive and generous. They should leave room for ordinary, unproductive time as well; a bond built only around achievement can become tiring.

## Potential Challenges

The Dragon's confidence can accidentally eclipse Rooster, especially in public. Rooster's corrections can then become a running commentary that makes Dragon defensive. Agreeing on how feedback is given, who gets credit, and when to stop polishing protects affection from becoming performance review.

## A useful everyday question

Before offering another improvement, each person can name what is already working. That is not empty praise: it gives the Dragon evidence that the Rooster sees the larger contribution, and it gives the Rooster permission to raise a detail without having to prove its value through relentless correction. A shared definition of “finished” is equally important in home projects and professional work.

## Beyond the zodiac animal

Dragon and Rooster are a Six Harmony year-sign association, not a complete BaZi verdict. Full chart work compares the two Four Pillars, Day Masters, Five Elements, and branch relationships, which may add support or tension that the animals cannot reveal. ## FAQ

### Are Dragon and Rooster compatible in love?

Traditional readings often see Dragon and Rooster as a capable Six Harmony match, with ambition balanced by precision. The symbolism is broad and cannot predict a couple's emotional safety, reliability, or long-term choices.

### Do Dragon and Rooster compete with each other?

They can if praise, credit, or decision-making is unclear. Turning feedback into a shared process and celebrating each person's distinct contribution makes their strong standards collaborative instead of competitive.
