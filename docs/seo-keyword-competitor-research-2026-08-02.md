# SEO Keyword and Competitor Research

Date: 2026-08-02
Market checked: Google US, English, personalization disabled

## Executive Summary

MyBaZiDestiny already has roughly the same sitemap scale as XuanSeal (about 182 versus 195 URLs). The immediate constraint is not page count. The stronger opportunities are:

1. make the three tool URLs useful as indexable reference pages, not only interactive forms;
2. assign one primary search intent to each tool and support article;
3. publish concise, verifiable passages that can be quoted in AI Overviews;
4. earn external mentions to supplement the site's young domain history.

No calculator, naming, compatibility, payment, or result-generation behavior is in scope for this SEO work.

## Competitor Findings

- `fsinmotion.com` was registered in 2012 and ranks strongly for calculator terms. Its calculator URL includes explanatory content and is cited in AI Overviews.
- `bazi-calculator.com` was registered in 2014 and benefits from an exact-match domain and a tightly focused tool.
- `xuanseal.com` was registered in December 2025, has about 195 sitemap URLs, and reached the first page for calculator and compatibility terms. This demonstrates that the category is not permanently controlled by old domains.
- The XuanSeal compatibility page combines a keyword-specific H1, visible methodology, related resources, WebApplication data, and visible FAQ content with FAQPage data.
- Birthday-based Chinese-name results are weaker and less authoritative than the BaZi calculator results. This is the clearest near-term opportunity for differentiated organic traffic.

## URL Ownership Rules

Do not create a second indexable page for the same intent without a clear distinction.

| Intent | Owner URL |
| --- | --- |
| Free BaZi calculation | `/calculator.html` |

| Chinese name generation | `/chinese-name.html` |
| Birthday-based naming method | `/chinese-name-based-on-birthday.html` |

## AI Overview Requirements

Each target page should contain:

- a direct 40-80 word answer near the relevant heading;
- precise headings that match real questions;
- visible methodology and limitations;
- definitions that do not depend on running JavaScript;
- links to supporting explanations and editorial standards;
- Article, WebApplication, BreadcrumbList, or FAQPage data only when the visible page supports it;
- language that frames BaZi as a traditional cultural system rather than scientifically proven prediction.

Structured data does not guarantee an AI Overview citation. It helps machines identify the page, while passage quality, indexing, corroboration, and external authority determine whether the source is selected.

## Measurement

Track weekly rather than daily:

- impressions and average position for each cluster;
- clicks and tool-start events by landing page;
- queries entering positions 1-10, 11-20, and 21-50;
- manual AI Overview citations for the monitored question set;
- branded searches for `MyBaZiDestiny`;
- external pages that mention or link to a target URL.

Google Search Console does not provide a dedicated AI Overview filter. A cited click generally appears as normal Google Web search traffic, so citations also require manual SERP monitoring.
